01

Overview

Calculating and estimating calories manually is time-consuming and challenging. The traditional method involves weighing and measuring food, and then comparing the values to calorie charts, which is difficult and not suitable for everyone. It's even harder for those with active lifestyles, as their daily caloric intake varies with their activities. Beginners may also struggle with this method, finding it inefficient and lacking the necessary tools. However, modern generations can easily calculate calories using just an image.

Manually calculating calories for complex local dishes like Mamak food is time-consuming and prone to error.
